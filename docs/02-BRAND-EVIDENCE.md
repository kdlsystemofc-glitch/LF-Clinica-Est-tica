### 2. LOGO & SYMBOL IDENTIFIER ANALYSIS

```
                 __  __     ____  _   _ 
                / / / /    / __ \| \ | |
               / / / /    | |  | |  \| |  (Mãe com bebê no colo - Line Art)
              / / / /___  | |__| | |\  |
             /_/ /_____/   \____/|_| \_|
             
         L U A N A   F E R N A N D E S
             C L Í N I C A   E S T É T I C A
```

1. **Monogram & Symbolic Isotype**:
   - **Cursive "lf" ligature**: Flowing, signature-like handwritten strokes denoting personal founder craftsmanship and intimate care.
   - **Mother & Child Line Drawing**: A continuous, delicate single-line contour drawing of a mother gently holding and gazing down at her newborn baby.
   - **Semiotics & Meaning**: This explicit symbol proves that maternal postpartum healing is the foundational soul of the clinic, elevating the brand far above generic cosmetic salons.
2. **Badge / Circular Seal Variant** (as evidenced in `imgi_63`):
   - A fine-line circular perimeter enclosing "LUANA FERNANDES" arched across the upper hemisphere and "CLÍNICA ESTÉTICA" along the lower hemisphere, with the monogram + mother/child illustration centered.
3. **Logotype Typography Hierarchy**:
   - **Brand Name ("LUANA FERNANDES")**: High-contrast, elegant serif / semi-serif typeface with refined proportions, uppercase structure, and generous letter-spacing (tracking ~+150–200).
   - **Sub-Brand / Category ("Clínica Estética")**: Clean, modern geometric sans-serif, uppercase, balanced tracking (tracking ~+200), providing clinical legibility and professional balance.

### 3. ENVIRONMENT & CLINICAL INTERIOR EVIDENCE

*Observed in `imgi_27`, `imgi_28`, `imgi_38`, and verified customer reviews.*

   - Brushed gold / satin brass metal legs on consulting chairs and procedure tables (`imgi_28`).

3. **Clinical Elements**:
   - White ergonomic aesthetic treatment beds with soft, warm coverings (polka-dot fleece drape).
   - Clean clinical white machinery and precision medical carts.
4. **Atmospheric DNA**:
   - A blend of clinical precision with boutique home-like hospitality (*"ambiente lindo, tem sempre um cafezinho e um bolinho pra nos receber"* — verified Google review).

### 5. TYPOGRAPHY CANDIDATES (IDENTITY PROFILE)

1. **Display & Headings (Elegance + Maternal Prestige)**:
   - Needs an exquisite serif or refined editorial semi-serif that mirrors the "LUANA FERNANDES" logotype.
   - *Candidates*: Cormorant Garamond, Italianno, Playfair Display, Cinzel, Oranienbaum.
2. **Body & Clinical Details (Clarity + Scientific Trust)**:
   - Needs a pristine, highly legible sans-serif that mirrors the "CLÍNICA ESTÉTICA" subtitle and social copy.
   - *Candidates*: Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, Outfit, DM Sans.
