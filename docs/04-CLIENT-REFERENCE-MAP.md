### 2. GEOMETRIC & EDITORIAL COMPATIBILITY SAFEGUARDS

1. **Handling 100% Vertical Assets**:
   - In Scene 04 (Carousel) and Scene 05 (Bento Tile 1), the client's vertical photos (`imgi_27`, `imgi_38`, `imgi_21`, `imgi_28`, `imgi_34`) fit naturally into vertical portrait card aspect ratios (4:5 and 9:16) without clipping or distortion.
2. **Text Metric Harmony**:
   - Portuguese copy was precisely drafted to match the character density of the Danish/English reference:
     - `systems / and / development` (3 lines) → `estrias / e cuidados / pós-parto` (3 lines)
     - `grand / ambitions` (2 lines) → `resultados / reais` (2 lines)
     - `sub / mer / ging` (3 chunks) → `re / cupe / rar` (3 chunks)
3. **Tone Calibration**:
   - Moves seamlessly from architectural minimalism to emotional maternal empathy without sacrificing the high-craft luxury posture of the reference.
